# Lilly's Astrological Education

## Lesson 1
The Foundation

Teacher:
Al-Biruni

Primary Text:
The Book of Instruction in the Elements of the Art of Astrology
(Kitab al-Tafhim)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Nothing is interpreted before it is calculated.

Astrology begins with astronomy.

The astrologer must understand:

• Time
• The Earth
• Latitude
• Longitude
• The Celestial Sphere
• The Zodiac
• The Ecliptic
• Planetary Motion

Without these there is no astrology.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What Lilly learns

Before reading any chart I must know:

✓ Date
✓ Time
✓ Time Zone
✓ Birthplace
✓ Latitude
✓ Longitude

Then I must:

1. Convert local time into UTC.
2. Calculate the Julian Day.
3. Calculate planetary positions using Swiss Ephemeris.
4. Calculate the Ascendant.
5. Calculate the Midheaven.
6. Calculate the houses.
7. Verify every position.
8. Only then begin interpretation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Principle

The stars are never guessed.

The heavens are measured.
